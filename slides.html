WPWarmPath

Find the Yale person who can actually introduce you, with a source for every claim.

Three agents on gpt-6-luna · about 13 seconds and $0.0018 a search

Cameron Hogg · AI Foundations final project · https://github.com/Cameronhogg2004/warmpath

The problem

  • Industry partnerships start with a warm introduction. US universities spent $6.36B of business-funded R&D in FY2024 (NSF 26-305)
  • Finding the right Yale introducer by hand is slow, and easy to get wrong:
"we would not ask a center director to introduce us to a company with whom they have no direct connection"

How the agents work

Scout (4 tools + optional web) → code checks → Verifier (LLM judge) → Writer → final scrub. All on gpt-6-luna, low effort.

Demo: Medtronic in 12 seconds

5 verified introducers, each tie linked to its trial or paper · draft intro email · live trace and token table

Cost (measured, not estimated)

$0.0018per run · 13,662 in (34% cached) / 1,693 out
$7.17per month, 100 users × 40 runs
$717per month, 10,000 users
  • Low vs medium reasoning: medium cost 1.7× as much, same lead introducers → kept low
  • Web-search tool: 2.2–3.3× the input tokens, 0 searches used → off by default
  • Prompt caching: 34% of input at 1/10 price

Safety: shown working

Red-team run (real agents, poisoned paper data): both injections removed before the Scout saw them; the fake person and emails appear nowhere

Direct injection refused: 0 tokens, $0

  • Evidence ledger + code checks + LLM judge
  • Emails only from a source
  • Caps: tools, requests, tokens, time, $/day
  • 20 offline tests

Impact

  • Real task, real reviewer: my MHS strategy needed 3 rounds over 2 days to get introducers with a current tie
  • WarmPath found 4 of 7 of my hand-checked introducers in about 13s per company, for under a cent
  • It agreed where the answer was "nobody" (Cigna), and repeated none of the rejected names
  • Users: NACRO's 550+ corporate-relations officers at 300+ institutions
